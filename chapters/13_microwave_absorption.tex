\chapter{Microwave absorption in the transmon}
\label{ch:microwave-absorption}

A transmon tuned to the Compton frequency
$\omega_q = m_\chi c^2/\hbar$ of a candidate ultralight bosonic
dark-matter particle absorbs the corresponding coherent dark-matter
field as a population transfer $\ket{0}\to\ket{1}$, providing a
single-microwave-photon-counting front end for dark-photon and
axion haloscopes in the GHz band. With photon-counting efficiency
$\eta\sim 0.5$ and dark-count rate
$\Gamma_\text{dc}\sim 1\,\text{mHz}$, the transmon-haloscope
combination reaches kinetic-mixing parameters
$\varepsilon\sim 10^{-15}$ and axion couplings into the QCD-axion
band $g_{a\gamma\gamma}\sim 10^{-15}\,\text{GeV}^{-1}$ for
multi-litre cavities in tesla-scale magnetic fields. This chapter
takes the absorption pipeline of \cref{ch:dm-search} and
instantiates it on this mechanism. The detailed treatment is in
preparation; this chapter is a placeholder.
