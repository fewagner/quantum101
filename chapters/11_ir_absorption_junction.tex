\chapter{Infrared absorption in the Josephson junction}
\label{ch:ir-junction}

A Josephson junction biased deep in the superconducting state
absorbs an infrared or terahertz photon of energy $\geq 2\Delta$
directly into the leads, breaking a Cooper pair and producing a
quasiparticle pair that briefly poisons the junction. The poisoning
event is observable as a transient shift of the qubit frequency or
of the junction's switching current. For a kinetically-mixed dark
photon of mass $m_{A'}\geq 2\Delta/c^2$, the same absorption
process converts the coherent dark-matter field into ordinary
photons that the junction then absorbs at a rate suppressed by
$\varepsilon^2$. This chapter takes the absorption pipeline of
\cref{ch:dm-search} and instantiates it on this mechanism. The
detailed treatment is in preparation; this chapter is a placeholder.
