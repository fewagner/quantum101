\chapter{Phonon-to-quasiparticle conversion}
\label{ch:phonon-qp}

In a superconducting absorber held well below its transition
temperature, an athermal phonon of energy at least the
Cooper-pair binding $2\Delta$ (e.g.\ $0.34\,\text{meV}$ in
aluminium, $\sim 1.4\,\text{meV}$ in niobium) breaks a Cooper pair
into two quasiparticles. The quasiparticle population diffuses to a
read-out element --- a transition-edge sensor or a microwave
kinetic-inductance detector --- where it is measured as a
small temperature or impedance change. This chapter takes the
generic recoil pipeline of \cref{ch:dm-search} and instantiates it
on this mechanism, deriving the energy threshold, energy
collection efficiency, and dark-matter mass reach of a
phonon-quasiparticle detector. The detailed treatment is in
preparation; this chapter is a placeholder.
