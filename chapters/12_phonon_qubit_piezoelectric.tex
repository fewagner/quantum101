\chapter{Phonon-qubit coupling via piezoelectricity}
\label{ch:phonon-qubit-piezo}

On a piezoelectric substrate (AlN, GaAs, or sapphire-based
sandwich heterostructures), a single phonon of frequency in the
1--10\,GHz band carries an electric polarisation that couples
linearly to a transmon's charge. The transmon thus operates as a
single-phonon detector, with the phonon playing the role that a
single microwave photon plays in cavity QED. Coupling rates of
order $g/2\pi\sim 0.1\text{--}10\,\text{MHz}$ are reachable, making
the transmon competitive with conventional kinetic-inductance
detectors above the sub-meV phonon-energy threshold. This chapter
takes the recoil pipeline of \cref{ch:dm-search} and instantiates
it on the piezoelectric phonon-qubit channel, with mass reach
extending below $1\,\text{MeV}/c^2$ for nuclear recoils. The
detailed treatment is in preparation; this chapter is a placeholder.
